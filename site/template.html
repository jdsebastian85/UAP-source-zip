About this index. Entity rows are regex candidates extracted from page text, not a verified roster. Nothing has been deleted: the default view hides rows mechanically flagged as fragments or over-captures, the count of hidden rows is stated above, and the toggle shows them with their flags. Every mention keeps its verbatim spelling and its release + page cite. Flag rules are published in scripts/normalize_entities.py and the collapse report. Alias expansion is a retrieval hint from a reviewed rules file: results are labeled “also matches” and never “same as”; clusters no person has reviewed yet say so.
